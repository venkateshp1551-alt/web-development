Match Guidelines and Stadium Rules

Entry Guidelines

  • Carry a valid match ticket for entry.
  • Carry a valid government-issued photo ID.
  • Arrive at the stadium well before match time.
  • Follow security checking procedures.

Prohibited Items

  • Outside food and beverages may not be permitted.
  • Weapons and sharp objects are prohibited.
  • Fireworks and dangerous materials are prohibited.
  • Professional cameras may require special permission.

General Instructions

  • Follow instructions given by stadium officials.
  • Do not damage stadium property.
  • Maintain cleanliness inside the stadium.
  • Do not enter restricted areas.
  • Tickets are subject to the stadium's terms and conditions.
